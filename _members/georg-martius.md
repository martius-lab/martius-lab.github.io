Group Leader and Head of the Department for Distributed Intelligence / Autonomous Learning.

I am a member of the Cluster of Excellence “Machine Learning: New Perspectives for Science” (see [website](https://uni-tuebingen.de/en/133840) / [blog](https://www.machinelearningforscience.de/en/)).

## Short CV

- **since April 2023**: Full Professor at Tübingen University, Department of Computer Science
- **2017 – March 2024**: Max Planck Research Group Leader, MPI for Intelligent Systems
- **2017**: Independent Post-doc Fellow at Institute of Science and Technology Austria
- **2015**: Post-doctoral position at Max Planck Institute for Mathematics in the Sciences, Leipzig, Germany
- **2010**: Post-doctoral position at Max Planck Institute for Dynamics and Self-Organization, Göttingen
- **2009**: PhD, Uni Göttingen and Bernstein Center for Computational Neuroscience (BCCN) Göttingen
- **2005**: University of Leipzig, Diploma in Computer Science
- **2003**: University of Edinburgh, Division of Informatics, Scotland, UK. Visiting student
